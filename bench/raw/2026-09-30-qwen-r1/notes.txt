Thinking mode: OFF (server-side)
  Qwen3.8-27B emits <think> blocks by default. Phase 0 FP8 answer contained a <think> block.
  Disabled server-side with --default-chat-template-kwargs={"enable_thinking":false} in all 6 manifests.
  Confirmed: no <think> block, no reasoning_content in any answer after the flag was set.
  The flag is in vLLM 0.24's --help output. This covers chat completions (captures, evals, smoke answers)
  and is ignored by the raw completions endpoint (benchmarks use --dataset-name random/sharegpt, which
  goes through the completions path, not the chat path, so thinking doesn't affect timing).
  Old FP8 answer (with thinking) saved as logs/phase0-fp8-answer-thinking-on.txt for evidence.

Warm-ups: 5 throwaway requests on every pod before any measured run.

GPU budget: 1 full H200 (Kueue quota nvidia.com/gpu: 1) + 1 MIG slice borrowed at a time (MIG quota: 0, borrow from unreserved cohort).
  Tested 2nd slice borrowing: "insufficient unused quota for nvidia.com/mig-3g.71gb, 1 more needed."
  Execution is two-lane: H200 lane (BF16, then MTP) and slice lane (INT4/71GB, then FP8/71GB, then INT4_35/35GB), sequential on each lane.

MIG slice NVML note:
  Both 71GB (mig-3g.71gb) and 35GB (mig-2g.35gb) slices fail CUDA graph profiling with
  "RuntimeError: NVML_SUCCESS == r INTERNAL ASSERT FAILED at /pytorch/c10/cuda/CUDACachingAllocator.cpp:1165"
  Workaround: --enforce-eager on all MIG slice deployments. Full H200 pods do NOT need this.
  All MIG-slice benchmarks and captures run with enforce_eager=True.
