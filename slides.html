Wrap-up

What to take home 🧳

The points we made

  1. On Hopper, try FP8 first. One GPU, BF16's speed, and no measurable loss on our tests.
  2. INT4 is for when the memory doesn't fit. 48 GB cards or more KV cache, at 89% of the speed; W4A16, so its 16-bit activations hold it back under load.
  3. Spec decode is an add-on. On BF16, 1.25× faster for one request; about half the tokens per GPU under load, which is draft overhead.
  4. No measurable quality loss. FP8 and INT4 matched BF16 on 1,319 GSM8K problems. 280 MMLU-Pro questions were too few to call, so test yours.
  5. A router lets each one run where it's best, once your traffic is big and mixed enough to run more than one.

🤔 Things to think about

  • How much of your traffic really needs full precision?
  • Is your traffic big enough to justify more than one pool?
  • Is your target cost per token, or time to answer?
  • Do you have an eval set built from real prompts?
